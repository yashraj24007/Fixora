🧪 IndexedDB Persistence Test

Verify that PDF documents persist across page refreshes

📋 Test Instructions

1 Open the Fixora App: http://localhost:8080/
2 Upload a PDF: Drag & drop or click to upload any PDF document
3 Wait for Processing: Wait until status shows "Ready" (green checkmark)
4 Refresh the Page: Press F5 or click the refresh button
5 Verify Restoration: Look for toast notification "📂 Documents Restored"
6 Check IndexedDB: Click the button below to inspect storage

✅ What to Expect

  • After upload, IndexedDB should contain: FixoraDB
  • 3 object stores: documents, chunks, embeddings
  • After refresh, documents auto-restore (no re-upload needed)
  • You can immediately ask questions without re-processing

🐛 Manual Testing with DevTools

  1. Open Chrome DevTools (F12)
  2. Go to Application tab
  3. Expand IndexedDB in the left sidebar
  4. Look for FixoraDB
  5. Check each store:
    • documents - Original PDF files with metadata
    • chunks - Text segments extracted from PDFs
    • embeddings - 384-dimensional vector embeddings
